# User Interview — Wishing Well

- **Who:** Friend / classmate
- **When:** 2026-07-29
- **How:** In person ~15 min

## What they do today (without your project)

They use a basic notes app to track wishes and goals. It's manual and doesn't provide any insights or reminders.

## What they liked

- Clean, simple interface
- Easy to add new wishes
- Visual progress tracking

## What confused them / what's missing

- Didn't understand how to categorize wishes
- Wanted reminders for deadline-based wishes

## What would make them actually use it

- Push notifications for wish deadlines
- Ability to share wishes with friends

## What I'll change (next steps)

- [ ] Add category selector when creating wishes
- [ ] Implement reminder system for deadline wishes
- [ ] Add share functionality
